About Us

KPR'n BIO FIT Health & Fitness Studio

KPR'n BIO FIT Health & Fitness Studio is a premier gym and physiotherapy establishment located in Viswanathapuram, Thudiyalur, Coimbatore. Founded on the belief that fitness should be accessible, personal, and results-driven, Bio Fit stands out for its highly knowledgeable and approachable trainers who provide individual attention to every member — without charging extra for personal training. With a perfect 5-star rating from nearly 500 members, the studio has earned its reputation as one of the best fitness destinations in the region.

At Bio Fit, we believe you don't need fancy machines to get fit — you need the right guidance, consistency, and a motivating environment. Our training philosophy centers around free weights, functional workouts, and bodyweight exercises that build real strength and endurance. Open six days a week from early morning until evening, and on Sunday mornings, we make it easy for you to stay committed to your health goals. Whether you're looking to lose weight, build muscle, recover from injury, or simply lead a healthier life, our expert trainers are here to guide you every step of the way.

  • Perfect 5-Star Rating from 488+ satisfied members — a true mark of trust and excellence
  • Individual guidance for every member with no extra charge for personal training
  • Early morning to evening hours (5:30 AM – 9:00 PM) to fit every schedule
